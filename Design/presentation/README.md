# 📊 Presentation & Pitch Deck Assets

This directory contains presentation slides, architectural flowcharts, and evaluation visual aids for hackathon pitch sessions and demonstration presentations.

## Recommended Pitch Deck Structure (5-Minute LeanInHack Demo)

1. **Slide 1: Problem Statement** — Rising epidemic of digital scams in India (₹1,750+ Cr lost in Q1 2024; electricity cutoff, digital arrest, fake bank KYC).
2. **Slide 2: The Core Flaw of Existing Solutions** — Naive regex blocklists fail on leetspeak (`w1n`, `lo@n`), Unicode homoglyphs, and multi-URL decoys.
3. **Slide 3: The ScamLens Architecture** — 4-tier multimodal defense: deterministic heuristics, 28 structural URL features, calibrated Random Forest models, and autonomous investigation agent.
4. **Slide 4: Live Demo (Mobile & Web)** — 1-click test scenarios showing real-time risk scoring, explainable reasoning, and immediate 1930 Helpline dispatch.
5. **Slide 5: Rigorous Evaluation & Holdout Validation** — 99.4% ROC-AUC on text, 99.1% on URLs, Platt calibrated probabilities, 0% data leakage.
6. **Slide 6: Team & Roadmap** — 4-member cross-functional ownership (Data & ML, Detection Logic, Integration & Backend, UI/UX).
